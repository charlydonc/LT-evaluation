💡 What This Tool Does

Lactate thresholds are key physiological markers that help determine your training zones and predict performance. LT1 marks the upper limit of your aerobic zone where lactate begins to accumulate, while LT2 represents your anaerobic threshold - the maximum intensity you can sustain for extended periods.

This calculator uses scientifically validated methods (Log-Log transformation for LT1 and Modified Dmax for LT2) to precisely identify these breakpoints from your lactate test data. Upload your test results to get accurate power-based training zones and track your progress over time.
